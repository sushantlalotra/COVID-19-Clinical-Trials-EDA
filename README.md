# COVID-19 Clinical Trials - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

This project explores global clinical trials related to COVID-19 to uncover trends, top contributors, study phases, and more. It provides visual insights into the state of research efforts and their evolution over time.

## 📂 Dataset
- **Source:** [Kaggle / ClinicalTrials.gov](https://www.kaggle.com/)
- **Contents:** Clinical trials data including sponsor info, country, trial status, start dates, phases, and study types.

## 🔍 Objective
To understand:
- Which countries and organizations are most active in clinical trials?
- What types of studies (interventional, observational) are being conducted?
- What are the common trial phases and statuses?
- How have trials evolved over time?

## 📊 Key Analyses & Visuals
- Top Countries by Number of Trials
- Sponsor Type Distribution (Industry vs Academic)
- Study Type Breakdown (Observational vs Interventional)
- Status & Phase of Trials
- Monthly Timeline of Trials Initiated
- Word Cloud of Trial Titles

## 🧠 Insights
- The United States leads in number of trials.
- Industry-funded trials dominate, but universities play a significant role.
- Majority of trials are interventional and in early-phase development.
- COVID-19 research peaked in mid-2020 and then stabilized.
